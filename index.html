About

Headshot, 4:5 portrait images/headshot/headshot.jpg

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at Virginia Tech, graduating in May 2027, and I like design work that ends in a physical prototype I can test. I'm currently team lead on Chiropter, a bat-inspired flapping-wing drone for senior design, and I've taken products from concept to prototype, from a microplastic-filtering kayak paddle to a new modular product at DockBloxx. My toolkit runs from CAD and simulation through 3D printing, CNC machining, and composites, with software as a bonus when it makes the hardware work easier. I'm looking for design-side mechanical engineering roles with hands-on prototyping.

About

I want to be an engineer because I want to make the world a better place and help people. A big part of that for me is designing things that help people get outside and fall in love with nature, the same way I did.

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Virginia Tech, graduating in May 2027, and I'm looking for a full-time role in mechanical design starting in summer 2027. I like owning a design from the first sketch to a part I can hold and test. That has been the common thread in my work so far: a flying wing glider I designed in Fusion 360 and built from balsa and carbon fiber, a kayak paddle that passively filters microplastics during a normal stroke, a modular dock product at DockBloxx that went from concept to the company's catalog, and now Chiropter, a bat-inspired flapping-wing drone I lead for senior design.

I also build software when it helps people do their jobs better. During my internship at Rhenus Automotive I created EasySDS, a web app that generates OSHA and GHS compliant chemical labels, which cut per-label cost to under a dollar and processing time by 90%.

The areas I'm most interested in are outdoor and bike products, sustainable energy, and additive manufacturing. Outside of engineering I spend most of my free time mountain biking and riding gravel. I also founded the hiking club at Virginia Tech and served as president of Hillel, a 400-student organization.

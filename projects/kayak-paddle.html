Overview

This kayak paddle passively filters microplastics from the water column during a normal stroke. It needs no power and asks for no change in the user's technique: filtering happens as a side effect of paddling.

The project secured development advising from an international venture capital firm focused on sustainability.

My Role

I led a small design team from concept through functional prototype, iterating the blade geometry with 3D printed test articles and on-water testing.
